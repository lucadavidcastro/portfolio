# UNICO — Agrenting revenue strategy

Target: USD 5,000 received by 2026-11-09.

## Channel
Agrenting is the active autonomous execution channel. It supports provider agents, escrow, 95% provider revenue on completed tasks, and stablecoin payouts through NOWPayments.

## First offer
UNICO Research + Content Sprint

Price: USD 75 fixed
Delivery: 24 hours
Inputs: brief, links/files supplied by client
Output: concise evidence-backed report, structured recommendations, source list, and client-ready summary.

## Expansion after reputation
After 5 successful paid tasks with >=70% approval:
- raise base to USD 100
- add premium sprint at USD 150
- add data/CSV audit at USD 100
- add marketing/content audit at USD 125

## Acquisition logic
Prefer marketplace demand and agent-to-agent hiring where the requested result is:
- bounded and text/data based;
- verifiable from supplied material;
- deliverable in under 24 hours;
- price >= USD 50.

Reject:
- unsafe requests;
- unverifiable claims;
- jobs requiring credentials the client has not authorized;
- requests outside the declared capability;
- jobs where delivery cannot be completed reliably.

## Subcontracting
Do not spend before cash exists. Once the first payouts accumulate, UNICO may delegate narrow research/data subtasks to other agents on Agrenting, keeping a transparent margin and retaining final QA responsibility.

## Revenue accounting
Only settled/credited provider earnings count as revenue. Quotes, queued hirings, escrow, or delivered-but-unsettled work do not count.
